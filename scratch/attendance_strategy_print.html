🎯 1. The 80k Unique Beneficiary Challenge & Telemetry Architecture

Across Madhya Pradesh's 52 districts, the total middle school teacher universe is ~80,000 teachers. Current monthly Cluster Level Shaikshik Samwaad (CLSS) averages ~23,000 attendees (~35%).

1.1 The Core Problem: Anonymous Aggregates vs. Unique Beneficiary Tracking
ARCHITECTURAL ROOT CAUSE
🔍 Analytical Diagnosis:
• Attendance is currently captured as aggregate cluster totals, while survey responses are collected separately on mobile forms without a mandatory unique employee identifier.
• Consequently, state leadership cannot determine whether the 23k monthly attendees are the same recurring teachers or a rotating cohort reaching new teachers.
🎯 Strategic Solution — Unique Identifier Standard:
Mandate the Treasury Employee Code (6–8 digits) paired with the School UDISE Code (11 digits) and Subject Domain (Math/Sci vs. Lang/Soc) as the primary check-in key. This immediately unifies CLSS cluster data and Block orientation logs into a single Master Teacher Telemetry Lake.

📱 4. Frictionless Feedback Architecture: Dynamic Cluster QR Codes
4.1 Dynamic QR Encoding & Pre-Populated URL Schema
SUBMISSION TIME: <75 SECONDS
🔍 The Current Problem: Teachers spend 8–10 minutes entering long Google Forms with 40+ questions on poor mobile connections, frequently mistyping District, Block, and Cluster names.
🛠️ The Engineering Architecture: Each Cluster Academic Center (CAC) receives a printed, laminated card with a unique QR code encoding:
https://rsk.mp.gov.in/clss/feedback?d=AgarMalwa&b=AGAR&c=CLUST04&f=FAC102&cyc=SEP2026
• 100% Pre-Populated (Zero Manual Entry): District, Block, Cluster, Facilitator, Date & Cycle Code.
• Teacher Inputs Only 2 Touchpoints: Treasury Employee Code + 5 Core Likert Star Ratings.
⚖️ 5. Represented Number Analysis & RSK Administrative Directives Playbook
Directive 1: Statewide Circular — Mandatory Rotational School Quorum
POLICY LEVER #1 • MANDATORY CIRCULAR
🔍 Field Telemetry Grounding: Schools currently send the same teacher repeatedly or send nobody due to lack of an explicit institutional mandate. Single-month turnout hovers at 35%.
📜 Executive Order Text:
Every Government Middle School with ≥2 teachers must depute at least one designated subject teacher to monthly Shaikshik Samwaad on a rotational basis (Cycle A: Math/Science; Cycle B: Language/Social Science). This guarantees 100% reach across the 80,000 universe in 2 cycles. Attending teachers must conduct a mandatory 30-minute peer-briefing at their school within 3 days.
Directive 2: Cluster Academic Coordinator (CAC) 100-Point Accountability Scorecard
GOVERNANCE LEVER #2 • MONTHLY CAC GRADING
🔍 Field Telemetry Grounding: Cluster performance varies wildly from >90% saturation down to <15% in low-monitoring zones (1,688 clusters had zero monitors present).
📊 Monthly 100-Point Scorecard Metrics:
1. Teacher Turnout Saturation (40 pts): ≥50% of mapped universe.
2. Unique Beneficiary Expansion (25 pts): ≥30% first-time / rotational teachers per cycle.
3. QR Digital Feedback Compliance (20 pts): ≥90% of attendees submit via QR code.
4. Punctuality & Session Adherence (15 pts): 30:70 talk ratio & on-time completion.
Directive 3: Automated 72-Hour Deficit Escalation to DPCs & BEOs
ESCALATION LEVER #3 • REAL-TIME BI ALERT
🔍 Field Telemetry Grounding: Zero-attendance schools currently go unnoticed until quarterly reviews, entrenching chronic non-participation.
🤖 Automated Protocol: Exactly 72 hours post-Samwaad, the BI engine detects all schools with 0 attendees and dispatches an automated WhatsApp & SMS escalation dossier directly to the concerned District Project Coordinator (DPC) and Block Education Officer (BEO) for mandatory follow-up.
