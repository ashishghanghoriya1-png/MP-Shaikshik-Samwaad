**Issue:** In multi-cycle capacity-building programs, displaying gross event turnout alongside net unique individuals where one metric is relegated to a small sub-text or footnote creates stakeholder confusion regarding actual cohort penetration versus total event touchpoints.

**Suggested improvement:** Establish the Dual-Metric Equality standard (`32px` equal typography prominence: `X Gross | Y Net Unique`) across cards, descriptions, and block directories whenever reporting longitudinal participation where candidates recur across cycles.

**Principle:** When reporting multi-cycle engagement where participants can recur across cycles, Gross Touchpoints and Net Unique Reach must share identical typographic weight to prevent misinterpretation of coverage versus volume.
