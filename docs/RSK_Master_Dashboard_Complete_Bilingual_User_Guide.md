## 1. Introduction & Purpose / परिचय और उद्देश्य

### English
The **RSK Madhya Pradesh Master Dashboard** is an executive-grade business intelligence portal tracking statewide capacity building, peer learning, and academic monitoring under the **Cluster-Level Shikshak Samvad (CLSS)** and **District Orientation (DO)** programs.

- **Primary Goal:** Track monthly attendance, teacher retention, subject-matter competency, and operational friction across all 52 districts in Madhya Pradesh.
- **Empirical Baseline:** 68,427 middle school teachers (Grades 6–8, Varg-2 Cadre) across 322 blocks and 3,014 Jan Shiksha Kendras (CRCs).

### हिन्दी
**राज्य शिक्षा केंद्र (RSK) मध्य प्रदेश मास्टर डैशबोर्ड** एक उच्च-स्तरीय रणनीतिक पोर्टल है, जिसका उद्देश्य **संकुल स्तरीय शिक्षक संवाद (CLSS)** एवं **जिला उन्मुखीकरण (DO)** कार्यक्रमों की प्रगति, उपस्थिति, शिक्षक निरंतरता और शैक्षणिक गुणवत्ता का सटीक विश्लेषण करना है।

- **मुख्य उद्देश्य:** मध्य प्रदेश के सभी 52 जिलों में मासिक शिक्षक उपस्थिति, प्रतिधारण दर, विषयगत समझ और प्रशासनिक चुनौतियों की रियल-टाइम ट्रैकिंग।
- **मूल आधार (Universe):** 322 विकासखंडों एवं 3,014 संकुल केंद्रों (जन शिक्षा केंद्रों) के अंतर्गत पदस्थ **68,427 माध्यमिक शिक्षक (वर्ग-2)**।

## 2. Global Slicers & Controls / ग्लोबल स्लाइसर और कंट्रोल

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ 📅 August 2026 │ 📅 September 2026 │ 🌐 Consolidated (Aug + Sep) │ 🇬🇧 English │ 🇮🇳 हिंदी │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### 1. Cycle Slicers (मासिक चक्र स्लाइसर)
- **📅 August 2026 (अगस्त 2026):** Shows single-month snapshot for August (23,785 teachers, 50 active districts, 2,897 active venues).
- **📅 September 2026 (सितंबर 2026):** Shows single-month snapshot for September (23,169 teachers, 52 active districts, 2,971 active venues).
- **🌐 Consolidated (Aug + Sep) [समेकित]:** Displays cumulative multi-cycle metrics deduplicated via unique teacher IDs (**46,954 Gross Touchpoints | 33,866 Net Unique Teachers reached, 49.5% Net State Reach**).

### 2. Role Filters (पद आधारित फिल्टर)
- **All (सभी):** Aggregates responses across all participants.
- **Teachers (शिक्षक):** Isolates attending teacher responses and pedagogical feedback.
- **Facilitators (सहजकर्ता):** Filters responses from Master Facilitators leading cluster sessions.
- **Monitors (अवलोकनकर्ता / जनशिक्षक / बीएसी):** Filters on-ground monitoring checklists and compliance scores.

### 3. Geographic Dropdowns (जिला व ब्लॉक चयन)
- Select any of the **52 Districts** or **322 Blocks** to drill down from statewide totals to individual local performance.

### 4. Language Switcher (भाषा परिवर्तन)
- Toggle between **English** and **हिन्दी** with a single click. All labels, cards, questions, and descriptions update instantly without page reloads.

### Tab 1: Overview & Summary / समग्र अवलोकन
**Purpose:** Top-level executive overview of statewide reach, active centers, and participation volume.

#### 6 Core KPI Cards Explained:
1. **Coverage Rate (कवरेज दर):** % of 52 revenue districts actively running Samwaad sessions (`96.2%` in Aug $\rightarrow$ `100.0%` in Sep/Consolidated).
2. **Training Centers / Venues (प्रशिक्षण केंद्र):** Total active physical venues (`3,018 Gross | 3,066 Unique Venues` = 3,014 CRC clusters + 52 DIET centers).
3. **Attending Teachers (उपस्थित शिक्षक):** Total middle school teacher turnout. Displays **Dual-Metric Equality** (`46,954 Gross | 33,866 Net Unique` = 49.5% Net Reach).
4. **District Orientation Participants (जिला उन्मुखीकरण प्रतिभागी):** District education leadership turnout (`8,888 Gross | 6,272 Unique Officers` with 58.7% cross-cycle retention).
5. **Trainers & Observers (सहजकर्ता व अवलोकनकर्ता):** Total support cadre (`10,724 Gross | 7,409 Unique` = 6,658 Facilitators + 681 Observers + 164 DO Leaders).
6. **Teacher Universe (शिक्षक संवर्ग आधार):** Total state middle school workforce (`68,427 teachers` from `Varg Wise Teacher Count.xlsx`).

## 4. Key Metrics & Calculation Formulas / मुख्य मैट्रिक्स व गणना सूत्र

| Metric / इंडिकेटर | Formula / गणना सूत्र | Description / विवरण |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Gross Touchpoints** | $\sum (\text{Aug Turnout} + \text{Sep Turnout})$ | Total session attendance instances across cycles (`46,954`). |
| **Net Unique Teachers** | $\text{Count}(\text{Unique Employee Codes})$ | Deduplicated unique human educators engaged (`33,866`). |
| **State Net Reach %** | $\frac{\text{Net Unique Teachers (33,866)}}{\text{Total Varg-2 Universe (68,427)}} \times 100$ | True workforce penetration rate (**`49.5%`**). |
| **Cohort Retention Rate %** | $\frac{\text{Repeat Champions (13,088)}}{\text{August Baseline Attendees (23,785)}} \times 100$ | % of August teachers who returned in September (**`55.0%`**). |
| **Facilitator Density** | $\frac{\text{Total Active Facilitators}}{\text{Total Active CRC Venues}}$ | Average facilitators per cluster venue (**`1.7`**). |

## 5. Visual Chart Interpretation Guide / चार्ट व ग्राफ को कैसे पढ़ें

### 1. Donut Charts (डोनट चार्ट)
- Center cutout displays total volume (e.g. `23,169 Teachers`).
- Arc segments represent proportionate stakeholder contributions with direct percentage labels.

### 2. Stakeholder Radar Chart (स्पाइडर / रडार चार्ट)
- 5 equidistant axes plotting scores from `0%` to `100%`.
- A balanced, expanded polygon indicates healthy multi-dimensional district performance. Dents highlight specific operational bottlenecks.

### 3. 2x2 Strategic Quadrant Scatter Matrix (रणनीतिक क्वाड्रेंट चार्ट)
- **X-Axis:** Operational Friction (Travel distance, resource strain).
- **Y-Axis:** Teacher Turnout % (Participation reach).
- **Interpretation:** Top-right represents High Turnout with High Friction (Q2 Resilient Frontier); top-left represents High Turnout with Low Friction (Q1 Benchmark).
